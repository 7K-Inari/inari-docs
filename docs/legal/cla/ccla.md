Thank you for your interest in contributing to 7K Group's Inari project ("We" or "Us"). The purpose of this contributor agreement ("Agreement") is to clarify and document the rights granted by contributors to Us. To make this Agreement effective after activation, an authorized representative must follow the electronic acceptance process described in [the CLA overview](README.md).

## How to Use This Agreement

Use this Agreement when a Legal Entity owns or controls the rights in Contributions Submitted by its personnel. The person accepting must have authority to bind the Legal Entity and the Affiliates whose Contributions are covered. Individual contributors who own all rights in their own Contributions should use the [Individual Contributor License Agreement](icla.md). Contact Us at [hello@7kgroup.io](mailto:hello@7kgroup.io) with questions about the process.

## 1. Definitions

"You" means the Legal Entity accepting this Agreement, on behalf of itself and its Affiliates whose Contributions are Submitted under this Agreement.

"Legal Entity" means an entity that is not a natural person.

"Affiliate" means any other Legal Entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the relevant Legal Entity. "Control" means (i) the power, directly or indirectly, to cause the direction or management of the entity, whether by contract or otherwise, (ii) ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of the outstanding shares or voting securities, or (iii) beneficial ownership of the entity.

"Contribution" means any original work of authorship, including any original modification or addition to an existing work of authorship, Submitted to Us by Your personnel or by another person authorized by You, in which You own the Copyright or for which You have authority to grant the licenses in this Agreement.

"Copyright" means all rights protecting works of authorship, including copyright, moral rights, and neighboring rights, as appropriate, for the full term of their existence.

"Material" means the software or documentation made available by Us to third parties. When this Agreement covers more than one software project, Material means the software or documentation to which the Contribution was Submitted. After a Contribution is Submitted, it may be included in the Material.

"Submit" means any act by which a Contribution is transferred to Us through tangible or intangible media, including electronic mailing lists, source code control systems, and issue tracking systems managed by or for Us, but excluding any transfer conspicuously marked or otherwise designated in writing as "Not a Contribution."

"Documentation" means any non-software portion of a Contribution.
