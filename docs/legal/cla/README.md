## Agreed Model

The drafts use the Harmony Contributor Agreement model with the following decisions:

- A **non-exclusive license grant**, not a copyright assignment. Contributors retain copyright.
- Broad rights for 7K Group to use, modify, distribute, sublicense, and re-license contributions.
- Individual and corporate variants, including authority requirements for corporate signers.
- A traditional patent license with a defensive-purpose revocation provision.
- No commitment to one perpetual outbound license. The current outbound license is Apache-2.0; possible future terms include AGPL, Business Source License (BSL), or proprietary terms.
- Digital click-through acceptance through CLA assistant, not PDF or email signatures.

Versions already distributed under Apache-2.0 remain available under that license. The re-licensing option applies to 7K Group's future exercise of the rights granted by contributors; it does not retroactively remove Apache-2.0 rights already granted to recipients.
